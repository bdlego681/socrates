Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Socrates, we take your privacy and the security of your supply chain data seriously. We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, build vendor profiles, or input inventory metrics. This includes account credentials, procurement logs, and vendor communication records.

2. How We Use Information

The primary use of your data is to power our algorithmic inventory models. We use historical procurement data to calculate real-time sales velocity and vendor risk metrics. Socrates does NOT sell your supply chain data to third-party data brokers or competitors.

3. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to protect your sensitive information. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Access to our infrastructure is strictly controlled and audited on a continuous basis.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy office at privacy@socrates.local.
