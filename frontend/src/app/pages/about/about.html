Our Story

Building the Future of Procurement

Socrates was founded in 2026 out of a simple observation: modern supply chains are incredibly complex, yet the tools used to manage them are stuck in the past. Our founders, veterans of global logistics and enterprise software, experienced firsthand the millions of dollars tied up in dead stock and the constant fire drills caused by unexpected stockouts.

We built Socrates to bring algorithmic intelligence to procurement—giving teams the power to automate the mundane and focus on strategic vendor relationships.
