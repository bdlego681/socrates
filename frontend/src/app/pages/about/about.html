The Origin

Socrates was born out of frustration. Our founders spent years managing procurement for major logistics hubs, watching teams struggle with spreadsheets, stale data, and gut-feeling decisions.

While supply chains had grown exponentially complex, the tools to manage them hadn't evolved since the 90s. The world needed a system that replaced guesswork with algorithmic certainty. We built Socrates to bring automated intelligence to the companies moving the physical world.
